Still building Monday client reports by hand from GA4, Meta and Google Ads?

  1. Morning digest, one message per client

    An agent with tools over GA4, Meta, Google Ads and the client’s other sources pulls the last 24 hours overnight and posts one short narrative per account. Every number links back to its source.

    Where a person decides: The account manager reads it before anything reaches the client. No budget moves without a person.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks
  2. Weekly client report drafted, not built

    The weekly deck becomes a drafted narrative with the charts attached, in your template, ready for the account lead to edit.

    Where a person decides: The account lead edits and sends. Nothing goes to a client automatically.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks
  3. Broken syncs caught before the client notices

    Expired tokens and stalled connections are detected and flagged the same morning, with non-expiring auth flows where the platform allows it.

    Where a person decides: A person fixes the connection; the system only alerts.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. 4 live integrations, one message at 07:00, every number linked to its source. Built for an in-house marketing team, the same Monday job agencies do by hand.

Marketing and advertising agencies · Creative production

Still waiting on one designer to review every ad variation?

  1. Ad creative generated and QA-checked on schedule

    Brief, cached prompt, image generation, then a vision model checks each image against the client’s written brand rules and regenerates on failure, capped.

    Where a person decides: A creative that fails twice is parked for review. A person approves the batch before it goes into the ad account.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Brand rules written down once, applied every time

    Logo placement, palette and text density become explicit rules the QA model enforces, so every creative carries its verdict and the rule it passed or failed.

    Where a person decides: The creative director owns the rules and can change them any time.

    AI systems2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Creative Generation Pipeline. 100+ QA-checked creatives a day at 78% lower inference cost, in production for an EU education brand.

Marketing and advertising agencies · New business

Still researching prospects one account at a time, by hand?

  1. Prospect research and outreach drafts, QA-gated

    Coordinated agents research a prospect, read its website, score it against your ideal client profile, find the opening and draft the outreach. A QA agent rejects weak drafts and the writer retries, capped at two attempts.

    Where a person decides: You review the QA-passed draft and press send. Rejected drafts show the reason.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Leads scored and routed into the CRM

    Inbound enquiries and researched prospects land in your CRM scored, with the research attached, so the first call starts informed.

    Where a person decides: Your team decides who to pursue; the score is advice.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. 6 agents, one shared state, retries visible in the UI, QA gate capped at two attempts.

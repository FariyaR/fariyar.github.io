Still closing the month from Shopify and 3PL exports that never agree?

  1. Shopify and 3PL data into SQL Server, reconciled nightly

    ShipHero or Shopify into staging tables, then stored procedures building silver and fact tables, with Power BI reading the fact tables. Voided shipments and double-counted orders handled explicitly.

    Where a person decides: A column-by-column parity audit runs with your finance or QC team before cutover. They sign off.

    Data systemsmulti-week
  2. Payouts reconciled against orders

    Processor payouts matched to orders, refunds and fees line by line, so the bank balance and the sales report agree.

    Where a person decides: Finance reviews listed mismatches; nothing is averaged away.

    Data systemsmulti-week

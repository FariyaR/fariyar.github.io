Still exporting the WMS every Monday to find out why the order count doesn’t match finance?

  1. WMS to SQL Server, reconciled every night

    ShipHero or your WMS into staging, stored procedures building silver and fact tables, Power BI on top. Voided and duplicated shipments handled explicitly, not filtered by hand.

    Where a person decides: Your QC or finance team signs off the parity audit before cutover. Rows that fail a check are queued with the reason.

    Data systemsmulti-week
  2. Client reports built and sent on schedule

    Per-client volume, SLA and billing reports generated from the fact tables and sent on the day they are due, with the numbers linked to their source.

    Where a person decides: The account manager reviews before the first send to each client; after that it runs unless a check fails.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: E-commerce Fulfillment ETL. Same data, same stack: ShipHero fulfilment data into SQL Server, 68,890 orders reconciled across 25 audited columns, nightly.

Still retyping packing lists and bills of lading from scanned PDFs?

  1. Packing lists and PODs read into validated records

    HTML, text PDFs and scans read by text and vision models, SKUs and customers matched through OCR noise, and a hard validation gate before anything reaches the WMS.

    Where a person decides: Records that fail the gate wait for a person with the reason attached.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Client order sheets imported, not keyed

    Spreadsheets in each client’s own layout mapped to your schema once, then imported on every arrival with the same checks.

    Where a person decides: New layouts are confirmed by a person before the mapping is saved.

    AI systems2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. 412 records, 0 validation errors on mixed HTML, PDF and scanned input. Tournament data, not freight paperwork.

Logistics, fulfilment and 3PL · RFQs and exceptions

Still quoting rates and chasing carrier exceptions by hand from a shared inbox?

  1. RFQs quoted from your rate card

    An RFQ arrives by email or PDF; the quote is drafted from your rates and lead times, in your template, and filed against the client.

    Where a person decides: A person approves every quote before it is sent.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks
  2. Carrier exceptions triaged and answered

    Exception emails are classified, matched to the order, and a reply or escalation is drafted with the order status pulled from your systems.

    Where a person decides: Replies to clients are approved by a person; the system drafts and files.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Drafting behind a QA gate is the LeadForge pattern; quote and RFQ drafting from email or PDF is a listed automation offer.

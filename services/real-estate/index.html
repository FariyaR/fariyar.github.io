Still answering portal enquiries one by one, after the lead has moved on?

  1. Enquiries scored, routed and answered with viewing slots

    Portal, email and WhatsApp enquiries are read, scored against your criteria, routed into your CRM, and a reply is drafted with viewing slots.

    Where a person decides: An agent approves every first reply. Enquiries below your line go to a person, not the machine.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks
  2. Viewing confirmations and follow-ups

    Confirmations, reminders and after-viewing follow-ups run on schedule and stop on reply, with outcomes written back to the CRM.

    Where a person decides: The agent sets the cadence and handles any reply.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Real estate and property management · Enquiries and viewings

Still answering portal enquiries one by one, after the lead has moved on?

  1. Enquiries scored, routed and answered with viewing slots

    Portal lead emails, website forms and WhatsApp enquiries are read, scored against your criteria and written to your CRM. A reply is drafted with viewing slots from the diary. Portals are read from the lead email they send; nothing is scraped.

    Where a person decides: A negotiator approves every first reply. Enquiries below your line go to a person, not the machine.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Viewing confirmations and follow-ups

    Confirmations, reminders and after-viewing follow-ups run on schedule by email, SMS or WhatsApp and stop on reply. Feedback is summarised and filed against applicant and property in the CRM, and a landlord or vendor update is drafted from it.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator sets the cadence, handles any reply and edits the landlord update before it goes.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Applicant matching for spreadsheet-run agencies

    For agencies keeping applicants in a spreadsheet or inbox; Reapit and Alto match natively. When a property goes live, applicants whose budget, area and move date fit get a drafted alert by email or WhatsApp, and the match is logged on both records.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator approves the send list before anyone is messaged.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Valuation requests answered and booked

    Market appraisal requests from your website form, portal lead emails and WhatsApp are logged to the CRM with the address and the owner's timing, a reply drafted with appraisal slots from the diary, and a reminder sent the day before.

    Where a person decides: A valuer approves the reply and the slot. No figure is ever quoted by the system.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Scoring against a profile and drafting behind a QA gate is the LeadForge pattern, built for B2B outreach, not lettings.

Real estate and property management · Tenant requests and maintenance

Still retyping tenant requests out of WhatsApp and chasing contractors by hand?

  1. Requests logged from WhatsApp, email and phone notes

    Each request from your WhatsApp Business number, email or phone notes is classified, matched to property and tenancy, and logged with photos in Fixflo, Arthur or Buildium, or by email and CSV where there is no open API. Photos set the suggested trade and urgency.

    Where a person decides: Urgent categories are escalated to a person immediately. The manager can override any suggested urgency.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Contractor dispatch and follow-up

    The right contractor is messaged with the job details and access notes by email or WhatsApp, chased until they confirm, and the tenant and landlord updated at each step from the log in your PM software.

    Where a person decides: Spend above a limit you set waits for approval.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Landlord approval requested with quote and photos

    When a job passes the landlord's limit, one message is drafted with the contractor's quote PDF, the tenant's photos, the repair history for that property and the contractor's earliest date. The reply, yes or no, is logged against the job.

    Where a person decides: The property manager sends it. The landlord's decision is read by a person before work is booked.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Contractor quotes and invoices checked against the job

    Quote and invoice PDFs from contractors are read, matched to the work order and property, and checked against the agreed rate and the landlord's approval limit before posting to Xero, QuickBooks or your PM software.

    Where a person decides: Anything over the limit or outside the rate waits for the property manager. Nothing posts on a guess.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. Multi-source intake with scheduled, logged actions is the OpenClaw shape; that system served a marketing team, not a property manager.

Real estate and property management · Leases, inspections and owner statements

Still typing deposit amounts and inspection dates in from scans?

  1. Leases and inspection reports read into the system

    Leases, inventories, inspection reports, IDs and proof of income, scans included, are extracted to names, dates, rent and deposit and pass a validation gate before Reapit, Alto, Arthur or Buildium is written to. Models can run on your own server or EU-resident infrastructure.

    Where a person decides: Documents that fail are queued with the reason for a person to check.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Owner statements from a reconciled ledger

    One pipeline: Reapit, Alto, Arthur, Buildium or AppFolio exports and your Xero or QuickBooks ledger into SQL Server via n8n, silver and fact tables, rent, invoices and bank lines reconciled line by line, owner statements in your template, every figure traceable to its source.

    Where a person decides: Your accounts team signs off a parity audit against current statements before cutover, then releases each month after reconciliation passes.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week
  3. AML and identity packs behind a validation gate

    For landlords, vendors and buyers: ID, proof of address and source of funds, as PDFs or photos, extracted into one schema, cross-checked for names, dates and expiry, and filed to the CRM with what is missing. Right to Rent packs for tenants where it applies.

    Where a person decides: Staff confirm identity and sign the risk assessment. The model assembles the pack; it never clears anyone.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Referencing results filed and next steps drafted

    Reports from Goodlord, Vouch, HomeLet or your screening provider arrive as PDF or email. Decision, conditions and any guarantor requirement are read and filed to the applicant in Reapit, Alto or your CRM, and the next message to applicant and landlord is drafted.

    Where a person decides: A person accepts or declines the applicant and sends both messages.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Landlord onboarding pack chased and filed

    Signed terms of business from DocuSign or Signable by webhook, the property form and bank details are read and written to the PM software after a validation gate. Missing items, certificates included, are chased by email on a schedule until the file is complete.

    Where a person decides: The property manager approves the landlord record before the property is marketed.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Messy scans into validated records, 412 records and 0 validation errors. Tournament data, not leases.

Real estate and property management · Listings and property marketing

Still writing every listing from scratch and finding feed errors from the portal email?

  1. Listing description drafted in your house style

    Photos, floor plan and CRM record in; a draft in your house style out, with bedrooms, floor area, rent and energy rating taken from the record, never guessed. Only worth it if the built-in copy in Alto, Reapit or Street does not sound like you.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator edits and approves. Nothing is published by the system.

    AI systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  2. Instagram and Facebook cards for new instructions

    When an instruction goes live, branded image cards are generated from your templates with the photos and facts from the record, checked by a vision model against your brand rules, regenerated on failure with a cap, and queued as a batch. Images only; no video.

    Where a person decides: A card that fails twice is parked. A person approves each batch before posting.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. CRM listings checked before the portal feed

    Before each feed push, every live record in your CRM is checked for missing photos, energy rating, price, status and availability. Rejection and expiry emails from Rightmove and Zoopla are read and matched to the record. No portal is scraped.

    Where a person decides: A person fixes the record; the check only alerts.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Slow listings flagged for a price review

    Weekly, enquiries per listing from the portal lead emails and viewings from the CRM are compared with your branch's usual rate at that price band. Listings falling behind are listed with the figures, and a vendor or landlord message is drafted in your wording.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator decides whether to raise a price change. The system never suggests a figure.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Creative Generation Pipeline. Image generation behind a vision QA gate with capped retries, 100+ a day. Built for an EU education brand, not an agency.

Real estate and property management · Rent, arrears and renewals

Still spotting late rent from a bank export and renewing tenancies at the last minute?

  1. Arrears reminders drafted, approved, then sent

    Late rent is picked up from the ledger in Arthur, Buildium, AppFolio or your PM software, by API or export. A reminder sequence in your wording goes by email, SMS or WhatsApp with amount and date filled in, and stops on payment or reply.

    Where a person decides: A person approves every reminder. Replies land with a person; nothing legal is sent automatically.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  2. Payment plan and landlord update drafted

    When a tenant replies, the thread is summarised, a payment plan proposed from your rules, and a landlord update drafted with the ledger extract attached, all filed to the tenancy.

    Where a person decides: The property manager agrees any plan. The landlord sees nothing the manager has not read.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Arrears file assembled for the solicitor

    Tenancy agreement, rent ledger, notices served and the correspondence thread are pulled from your PM software and mailbox into one dated pack with a timeline, ready for the solicitor or the landlord's rent guarantee insurer. The timeline summary is the only model step.

    Where a person decides: The property manager and landlord decide whether to proceed. The pack is evidence, not advice.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Renewal offers drafted ninety days out

    Tenancies ending in ninety days are listed from the PM software. A renewal proposal is drafted with comparables from your recent lets in the CRM and any market report you upload as PDF, sent to the landlord for a decision, then to the tenant.

    Where a person decides: The landlord signs off the rent. The property manager sends the tenant offer; no notice is served automatically.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Renewal paperwork signed and deposit details prepared

    Once terms are agreed, the renewal agreement is generated from the record, sent through DocuSign, Signable or Goodlord, and the signed copy filed. The new rent and end date are written to the PM software, and the deposit re-registration details prepared for your scheme.

    Where a person decides: The property manager checks the agreement before it is sent and submits the deposit scheme update.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. Every figure in the message linked to its source row, on schedule, unattended: the OpenClaw mechanics, built for marketing numbers, not rent.

Real estate and property management · Certificates, inspections and compliance

Still tracking gas, electrical and energy certificate expiries in a spreadsheet?

  1. Certificates read, expiry dates written to the property

    Gas safety, electrical, energy and fire certificates arrive as PDFs and photos from contractors. Dates, addresses and outcomes are extracted, matched to the property through OCR noise, and written to Arthur, Fixflo, Buildium or your PM software after a validation gate.

    Where a person decides: Anything that fails the gate waits for a person with the reason. A failed certificate always reaches a human.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Renewals booked with contractors before expiry

    Sixty, thirty and seven days before expiry the contractor is messaged, chased until confirmed, the tenant offered access dates by email or WhatsApp, and every step logged against the property in your PM software. Your PM tool reminds; this one chases.

    Where a person decides: The property manager approves the contractor and any cost above the usual rate.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Compliance position per landlord, on schedule

    A scheduled report per landlord and per branch: what is in date, what expires this month, what is missing, every line linked to the certificate it comes from. Sent by email or posted to Slack.

    Where a person decides: The compliance lead reads and decides. The report changes nothing.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Contractor insurance and registration checked before dispatch

    Public liability certificates and Gas Safe, NICEIC or local registration documents from each contractor are read as PDFs, expiry dates written to the contractor record, and renewals requested by email. Dispatch to a contractor with a lapsed document is held.

    Where a person decides: The property manager can release a held job. Registration is confirmed by a person, not the model.

    AI systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Routine inspections booked and issues raised

    Inspections due are listed from the PM software. The tenant is offered dates by email or WhatsApp, the visit booked in the diary, and the report PDF from Inventory Hive, InventoryBase or your template read so each defect becomes a maintenance request on the property.

    Where a person decides: The inspector confirms the defect list before any request is raised or a landlord told.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Certificates are scans in inconsistent layouts, the shape of the 412-record run with 0 validation errors. Tournament data, not certificates.

Real estate and property management · Offers and sales progression

Still chasing solicitors and brokers from a notepad between offer and completion?

  1. Offers logged with proof of funds attached

    Offers arriving by email, portal lead email or phone note are recorded on Reapit, Alto or your CRM with the buyer's position, chain and proof of funds read from the PDF they send. A vendor summary with every offer side by side is drafted.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator presents offers. Nothing reaches the vendor unread, and no offer is accepted by the system.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  2. Sale-agreed pack sent to both sides

    When an offer is accepted, the memorandum of sale is drafted from the CRM record and sent to both solicitors or attorneys and the broker, with acknowledgements chased. Key dates and the chain are logged on the sale.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator checks the memo before it goes.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Solicitors, surveyors and brokers chased to completion

    One typed file per sale: searches, survey, mortgage offer, enquiries, contract, completion. Chasers go by email on schedule and stop on reply; replies are read, the milestone updated on Reapit, Alto or your CRM, and every draft checked against the file by a QA step.

    Where a person decides: The progressor sends every chaser. Dates are proposed, never agreed, by the system; failed drafts are parked.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week
  4. Weekly update to vendor and buyer

    Each week a progress note per sale is drafted from the logged milestones in your wording: what moved, what is waiting, who holds it, the chain in one line. Sent by email or WhatsApp after approval.

    Where a person decides: The negotiator approves each note. Nothing about price or legal advice goes out.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Stalled sales flagged before they fall through

    A scheduled list of sales with no milestone movement in the number of days you set, missing documents and survey issues, with the reason taken from the last reply in the thread. Posted to Slack or emailed to the branch manager.

    Where a person decides: The branch manager decides who to call. The list changes nothing.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. One typed state per sale, drafts checked before anything is sent: the LeadForge mechanics, built for B2B lead research, not conveyancing.

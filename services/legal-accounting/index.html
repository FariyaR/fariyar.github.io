Legal and accounting firms · Document intake and filing

Still opening every client PDF by hand to find out which matter it belongs to?

  1. Documents filed to the right client and matter

    Attachments, scans and portal uploads are read, matched to the client and matter in Clio, Actionstep or LEAP with a resolver built for OCR noise, and filed after a validation gate. Duplicates and later versions are recognised and filed as one record.

    Where a person decides: Anything that fails the gate waits for a clerk with the reason. Nothing is filed on a guess.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Key fields extracted into the matter record

    Names, dates, amounts and deadlines pulled from the document into your practice system in a fixed schema, so the record is searchable and the deadline is in the calendar.

    Where a person decides: Deadlines are proposed, never set, without a person confirming.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. The intake pipeline deployed in your own stack

    The same intake, extraction and filing pipeline deployed as a product in your environment: a vision model for scans, the resolver, the validation queue and the clerk's review screen, on hardened EU-resident infrastructure or your Azure tenant, with open-weight models, so client documents never leave.

    Where a person decides: You set the residency rules before the build and sign off the deployment.

    AI productsComplex system · multi-week
  4. Scanned post split, read and routed

    The filing and extraction services above, applied to the post room's morning scan batch. The new work is splitting the batch into separate letters and routing each to the right fee earner, with court and tax dates flagged for the diary.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner confirms any date before it enters the diary.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. The same mechanism: messy documents in, 412 records out with 0 validation errors. Built for tournament data, not a firm.

Legal and accounting firms · Client onboarding and AML checks

Still chasing ID documents and typing engagement letters for every new client?

  1. Engagement letter and onboarding chase from intake

    The website or Typeform intake becomes an engagement letter in your template, with scope, fees and the right terms, sent for e-signature through DocuSign. Reminders to sign, upload ID and pay money on account run on schedule and stop as each step completes.

    Where a person decides: The partner approves every letter before it goes out; a client who replies with a question reaches a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  2. IDV results filed and matched to the intake

    The identity check itself runs in Thirdfort or Credas. Their result arrives by webhook or as the PDF report, is matched against the name, address and date of birth on the intake, filed to the client record, and any mismatch or expired document is flagged.

    Where a person decides: Your MLRO or compliance lead makes the decision; the system files and compares.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Conflict search beyond the practice system's own

    Clio, LEAP and Actionstep ship a basic conflict search. This adds spelling variants, related parties and company officers from the register, and the correspondents in your Outlook mailboxes through the Microsoft Graph API, listing every hit with the matter it came from.

    Where a person decides: A fee earner reviews the hits and clears the conflict.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Company register lookups attached to the file

    Company number, officers, persons with significant control and filing dates pulled from the free Companies House API, or your national register's public API, and attached to the client record, with mismatches against the intake flagged.

    Where a person decides: The compliance lead reads the flags; nothing is accepted automatically.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Risk assessment pre-filled, never scored alone

    The client risk form pre-filled from the IDV result, the register data and the matter type, with each answer linked to its source document.

    Where a person decides: The MLRO assigns the risk rating. The system fills, it does not rate.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Research, drafting and a QA gate before anything ships is the LeadForge pattern, built for B2B outreach, not client onboarding.

Legal and accounting firms · Client inbox and calls

Still answering “what’s the status?” by hand, as unbilled time?

  1. Status replies drafted from the matter record

    Routine questions get a reply drafted from the matter or engagement record in Clio, Karbon or your practice system: what is done, what is due, what is needed from the client.

    Where a person decides: Every outbound message is approved by a person. Anything that reads as advice goes to the fee earner.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Document requests and reminders that stop on reply

    Missing-document requests and deadline reminders run on schedule from the record, by email or client portal, and stop when the client responds.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner sets what is chased and when.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Inbox triaged into matters by urgency

    The filing service from intake, applied to the shared or personal Outlook inbox through the Microsoft Graph API. The new part is the triage: each email tagged urgent, routine or newsletter and the fee earner's morning list sorted that way.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner decides what to answer; the system sorts and files.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Calls and voicemails into the file, self-hosted

    Call recordings from 3CX, RingCentral or Teams, pulled by webhook or export, transcribed on self-hosted speech models on your own GPU, summarised into an attendance note and filed to the matter with the duration for time recording. No audio reaches a third-party API.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner edits and signs the note before it is filed.

    AI productsComplex system · multi-week
  5. WhatsApp messages captured to the matter

    Client messages on a firm number through the WhatsApp Business API, not fee earners' personal phones, are filed to the matter with attachments, acknowledged from a template out of hours, and listed for the fee earner each morning.

    Where a person decides: Replies with substance are written by the fee earner.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. Scheduled, source-linked messages are the OpenClaw analytics agent pattern, built for a marketing team rather than a firm.

Legal and accounting firms · Drafting and document review

Still starting every draft from last year’s file and reviewing contracts clause by clause?

  1. First drafts beyond the merge fields

    Clio and LEAP already fill merge fields. This drafts the bespoke paragraphs: letters, agreements and accounts cover letters written from your own precedents and the matter or engagement record, with the facts taken from the system rather than retyped.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner edits and approves. Drafts are marked as drafts until then.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Contracts reviewed against your playbook, in your stack

    An incoming contract is split into clauses, each compared with your playbook positions, and deviations listed with the clause, your fallback and the risk level you assigned. It runs on EU-resident or self-hosted infrastructure, for firms that vetoed off-the-shelf review tools on residency.

    Where a person decides: The lawyer decides what to negotiate; the review is a checklist, not advice.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week
  3. Clause and term extraction into a schedule

    Parties, dates, notice periods, amounts and renewal terms extracted from lease, loan or supply agreements into a fixed schema and a schedule you can sort in Excel. The output is one sortable schedule across many agreements, not a record per document.

    Where a person decides: Fields that fail a check are queued for a person with the reason.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Draft accounts and tax computations checked before sign-off

    Draft statutory accounts and corporation tax computations compared with the ledger export from Xero, QuickBooks or Sage and with last year's file, with differences, missing disclosures and unexplained movements listed for the reviewer.

    Where a person decides: The reviewer signs off; the system never files or adjusts.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Proof-read against the house style

    Word drafts checked for defined terms, cross-references, numbering, dates and your house style before they leave, with each issue listed by paragraph and offered as tracked changes. Docx parsing plus rules, so the checks are repeatable.

    Where a person decides: The author accepts or rejects each change.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Contract pack and search results checked

    In conveyancing, the title register, TA6 and TA10 forms, and the search result PDFs are read into a fixed schema and checked against your pre-exchange checklist: restrictions, charges, planning hits and gaps between the forms and the register, listed for the fee earner.

    Where a person decides: The conveyancer raises the enquiries; the list is a prompt, not advice.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Extraction into a fixed schema with deterministic checks after the model: 412 records, 0 validation errors. Tournament data, not contracts.

    An agent reads the day's sent email, calendar, call notes and edited documents, only for the mailboxes and date range you authorise through the Microsoft Graph API on your tenant, and proposes time entries against the matter with a narrative in your style.

    Where a person decides: The fee earner accepts, edits or rejects every entry before it posts.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Bills drafted from WIP in your narrative style

    At the billing date, unbilled time and disbursements in Clio, Xero or your practice system become a draft bill with narratives tidied, grouped by phase and written in the style of your past bills.

    Where a person decides: The partner approves every bill. Nothing is issued by the system.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. WIP, lock-up and aged debt every Monday

    A short scheduled message per team: unbilled WIP, lock-up days, bills over 30 and 60 days, with each number linked to the source in Clio or Xero.

    Where a person decides: The managing partner reads it; the system changes nothing in the ledger.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Fee escalation and money-on-account top-ups

    Xero and Clio already send the first reminders and stop on payment. This adds what they lack: partner-approved escalation steps in your wording, money-on-account top-up requests when a matter's balance falls below the agreed float, and reminders for unpaid disbursements.

    Where a person decides: A partner approves each escalation; a reply reaches the fee earner.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Fixed-fee overruns flagged before the deadline

    A nightly query compares time recorded against fixed-fee matters with the agreed budget field in your practice system, and flags matters past 70 per cent to the supervising partner.

    Where a person decides: The partner decides whether to re-scope; the flag only informs.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Legal and accounting firms · Client bookkeeping and VAT

Still coding every client’s receipts and bank lines before the VAT return can start?

  1. Line-level coding proposed after Dext capture

    Dext and Hubdoc capture the receipt and apply one rule per supplier. This proposes the nominal code and VAT treatment per line, from the client's past coding, the chart of accounts and the line description, with a reason attached, posted as a draft.

    Where a person decides: Low-confidence codings go to the bookkeeper; the rest wait as drafts for review.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Unmatched bank lines queued per client

    Xero, QuickBooks and Sage match the easy bank lines. This matches the rest with escalating rules against invoices and receipts, and the value is the queue: every unmatched line listed per client each week, with the closest candidates and the reason it failed.

    Where a person decides: The bookkeeper clears the unmatched list; nothing is reconciled on a guess.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Client query lists sent and chased

    Unexplained transactions become a query list sent to the client in your template, answered through a link rather than a reply email, and chased until complete, with the answers landing against the transaction.

    Where a person decides: The bookkeeper reviews answers before anything is posted.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. VAT and MTD ITSA pre-check across the ledger

    Before each VAT return or MTD ITSA quarterly update, the period's transactions in Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent are checked by rules for rate errors, missing invoices, duplicates, out-of-period items and uncategorised lines, with a list per client.

    Where a person decides: The accountant reviews and submits through your tax software; the system never files.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Management accounts pack per client, on schedule

    A monthly or quarterly pack from the ledger with commentary drafted on the movements, in your own template rather than Fathom's or Syft's, for every client on the list.

    Where a person decides: The client manager edits the commentary and sends.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Bookkeeping for many clients, in one pipeline

    All client ledgers synced into SQL Server, with coding rules, exception queues and Power BI across the whole client base: who is behind, where queries pile up, hours per client.

    Where a person decides: The practice manager signs off the parity audit before anyone relies on it.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week

Closest system I’ve shipped: E-commerce Fulfillment ETL. Matching platform lines to a ledger with a parity audit is the ETL pattern: 68,890 orders across 25 audited columns. Retail, not client books.

    Year-end source documents, forwarded by email or uploaded as PDFs and scans, are read into the return schema: employment income and tax, dividends, bank interest, pension contributions, each figure linked to its page, and dropped into the client's tax file for the preparer.

    Where a person decides: The preparer checks each figure against the page before it enters the return.

  2. Missing items per client from last year's return

    What has arrived for each client is compared with the sources on last year's return, so the gap list is built rather than remembered: the P60 for the second job, the interest certificate, the dividend vouchers. The chase runs through the client inbox reminders.

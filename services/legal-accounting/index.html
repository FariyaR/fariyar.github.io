Legal and accounting firms · Document intake and filing

Still opening every client PDF by hand to find out which matter it belongs to?

  1. Documents filed to the right client and matter

    Incoming attachments, scans and PDFs are read, matched to the client and matter with a resolver built for OCR noise, and filed only after a validation gate.

    Where a person decides: Anything that fails the gate waits for a clerk with the reason. Nothing is filed on a guess.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Key fields extracted into the case or engagement record

    Names, dates, amounts and deadlines pulled from the document into your practice system in a fixed schema, so the record is searchable and the deadline is in the calendar.

    Where a person decides: Deadlines are proposed, never set, without a person confirming.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  3. Everything in your own stack

    Models can run on hardened, EU-resident infrastructure so client documents never leave your environment.

    Where a person decides: You set the residency rules before the build.

    AI products4–8 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. The same mechanism: messy documents in, 412 records out with 0 validation errors. Built for tournament data, not a firm.

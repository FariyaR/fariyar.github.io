  1. CVs in any format read into the ATS

    PDFs, Word files, scans and LinkedIn exports extracted, names, dates and employers normalised with matching built for scan noise, then a validation gate before your ATS is written to.

    Where a person decides: Files that fail wait with the reason; nothing is dropped silently.

  2. Shortlist scored against written criteria

    Each candidate scored against the job criteria written down once, with the evidence for each score shown next to it.

    Where a person decides: A recruiter reviews the scored shortlist before anyone is contacted or sent to a client.

Still chasing candidates one WhatsApp at a time, between calls?

  1. Next message drafted from the job and the history

    Replies across email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn are read, the next message is drafted from the job and the candidate’s history, follow-ups are scheduled and stop on reply, status written back to the ATS.

    Where a person decides: Every outbound message waits for the recruiter’s approval. Offers and rejections are never sent automatically.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks
  2. Interview scheduling without the back-and-forth

    Slots offered from the calendar, confirmations and reminders sent, and the ATS updated when the interview is booked.

    Where a person decides: The recruiter confirms any interview a client must attend.

    Automation3 days – 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Research, scoring and drafting behind a QA gate capped at two attempts, built for B2B outreach rather than candidates.

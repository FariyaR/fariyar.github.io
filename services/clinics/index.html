Healthcare clinics · Patient enquiries and booking

Still answering “anything this week?” on WhatsApp between patients?

  1. Enquiries answered from your FAQ and price list

    One flow over the WhatsApp Business API, Instagram DMs and email drafts replies from your FAQ and price list, offers open slots from Dentally, Cliniko or Pabau through their APIs, and logs each enquiry with its source and outcome.

    Where a person decides: The front desk approves every reply before it sends. Anything clinical goes to a person, never the machine.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Reminders and recalls on schedule

    Appointment reminders and hygiene recalls run from Dentally, Cliniko or Pabau on a schedule, by WhatsApp or SMS, in your wording, and stop as soon as the patient replies or books.

    Where a person decides: You choose the wording and timing; replies always reach a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Reviews asked for at the right moment

    After a completed visit, a Google review request goes out in your words, once. Every patient is asked; the only rule is a delay while a complaint is open, not a filter on who is asked.

    Where a person decides: The practice manager sets the timing and the delay; the system never decides who deserves a request.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Missed calls answered on WhatsApp within minutes

    Needs a phone system with webhooks: Aircall, 3CX or RingCentral. A missed call or voicemail triggers a WhatsApp message with a booking link, the voicemail is transcribed, and the enquiry is logged with its source.

    Where a person decides: The desk sees every transcript; callers who ask for a person get a callback task.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Cancellations filled from the waitlist

    When a slot opens in Dentally, Cliniko or Pabau, matching patients on the waitlist are offered it in order by WhatsApp or SMS. The slot is held while each offer is open, and the first to confirm is booked.

    Where a person decides: You set the order and the rules for which patients are offered what.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Calls answered and booked by a voice agent

    Calls forwarded from Aircall, 3CX, RingCentral or a Twilio number reach an agent that books, moves and cancels appointments through the Dentally or Cliniko API and hands anything else to a person. Speech models run on your own GPU; call audio stays with you.

    Where a person decides: Every call is transcribed and reviewed in the first weeks. Anything clinical or unclear goes to the desk at once.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week

Closest system I’ve shipped: DentaSmile AI. DentaSmile runs in production for dental clinics; the enquiry flows and the voice agent are new offers, not yet a case study.

Healthcare clinics · Intake documents and referrals

Still retyping intake forms and referral letters from photos into the practice system?

  1. Intake forms and referrals read into the record

    Intake forms, GP referrals and insurance letters, as photos, scans or PDFs, are read by text and vision models, matched to the patient record, and checked against hard rules. Clean records go to Dentally or Cliniko by API, or to an import file.

    Where a person decides: Records that fail the validation gate wait for a staff member with the reason.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Patient data kept in your stack

    The same intake pipeline on hardware you control: OCR and an open-weight vision model on a RunPod GPU or your own server, EU-resident where required, the DentaSmile pattern. Patient documents and photos never reach a third-party API. Same validation gate, same queue.

    Where a person decides: You decide where data may go before the build starts.

    AI productsComplex system · multi-week
  3. Medical history forms checked before the appointment

    Online or paper medical history forms are read, compared with the previous version in the record, and changes in medication, allergies or conditions are flagged on the day sheet before the patient is seen.

    Where a person decides: The clinician reads the flags; nothing is interpreted clinically by the system.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Referrals triaged and acknowledged the same day

    For specialist practices. Once a referral is extracted, it is classified by treatment type and urgency from your rules, acknowledged to the referrer from a template by email, and queued for the right clinician.

    Where a person decides: A clinician sets the urgency rules and confirms anything marked urgent.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Duplicate patient records found once, from the export

    A one-off clean-up. The patient export from Dentally, SOE Exact or Cliniko goes through the escalating matcher that resolves misspelt names, old addresses and swapped dates, and comes back as a merge list with a confidence per pair.

    Where a person decides: The desk confirms each merge in the practice system; nothing is merged by the system.

    Data systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. 412 records, 0 validation errors on mixed PDFs and scans, with a hard validation gate. Tournament data rather than patient records.

Healthcare clinics · Patient-facing previews

Still asking cosmetic patients to commit without seeing their own result?

  1. A preview app in the shape of DentaSmile

    The patient uploads a photo, a self-hosted open-weight model generates the preview on RunPod or your own GPU, and a Next.js app with a FastAPI job API queues and polls instead of timing out. Workers scale with the queue under a spend cap you set.

    Where a person decides: The clinician reviews the preview with the patient. The treatment decision stays with the clinician.

    AI productsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Previews logged from upload to result

    Each job keeps the input photo, the preview, its status and timing, and who viewed it, under retention rules you set. Patient deletion requests are run from one place and logged, so you can show what a patient was shown and when.

    Where a person decides: Your team decides retention and who can see the log.

    AI productsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Preview on your website as the first enquiry

    The preview flow embedded on your site: photo in, preview out, with consent recorded before upload and the enquiry filed to HubSpot, Pipedrive or your CRM with the image attached, so the desk follows up from the record.

    Where a person decides: You decide what the patient sees; the desk follows up every enquiry.

    AI productsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Previews for more than one treatment

    Whitening, aligners, veneers or skin treatments as separate preview modes on the same worker, each with its own prompt, test set and review rules, so a cosmetic clinic can offer more than one result from one upload.

    Where a person decides: The clinician signs off each mode on test photos before it is offered to patients.

    AI productsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: DentaSmile AI. Built for dental clinics and live: 3 services, ~110 s per inference, queued and polled, photos kept off third-party APIs.

Healthcare clinics · Treatment plans, consent and aftercare

Still letting treatment plans sit unaccepted because nobody has time to follow up?

  1. Treatment plan letter drafted from the plan

    The plan, from the Dentally API or the plan export or PDF from SOE Exact or Dentrix, becomes a patient letter in plain language, in your template, with costs, stages and the finance options you offer.

    Where a person decides: The clinician approves every letter. Clinical wording is never changed by the system.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Consult follow-ups that stop on reply

    After a consultation or an unaccepted plan, a sequence by WhatsApp or email in your words, spaced as you choose, that stops the moment the patient books, pays a deposit or replies. Patients who never reply are listed for the coordinator.

    Where a person decides: The treatment coordinator owns the wording and takes every reply.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Finance quote and deposit sent together

    The plan cost split into the payment options you run, with the deposit link from Stripe or GoCardless and the booking slot in one message. Finance terms and monthly figures come from your finance provider's table, never from the system.

    Where a person decides: The desk approves the quote; terms come from your finance provider, not the system.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Consent forms matched to the treatment

    The right consent form for the booked treatment, read from the Dentally API or the day-sheet export, is sent for e-signature before the appointment. The signed copy is filed to the record and checked against what was booked.

    Where a person decides: A missing or mismatched consent is flagged to the clinician before the appointment starts.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Pre-op and post-op instructions by procedure

    Instructions matched to the booked procedure go out by WhatsApp or email at the right time before and after the appointment, with a check-in message after surgery that routes any concern to the desk.

    Where a person decides: Clinicians write the instructions; any reply describing a problem goes to a person at once.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Drafting behind a QA gate with capped retries is the LeadForge pattern, built for B2B outreach rather than treatment plans.

Healthcare clinics · Clinical notes and letters

Still writing up notes after the last patient and referral letters at the weekend?

  1. Dictation into your note template, self-hosted

    The clinician dictates after the appointment. A self-hosted speech model on your GPU transcribes, and the text is filled into your note template; audio never leaves your servers. Summarising or structuring clinical content is out of scope.

    Where a person decides: The clinician reads and signs every note. Nothing is filed unsigned.

    AI productsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Referral and GP letters drafted from the notes

    A referral or discharge letter drafted from the signed note and the patient record, on your letterhead, in your wording, ready to file in the practice system and send. Clinical findings are copied, not rephrased.

    Where a person decides: The clinician approves each letter before it is sent.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Phone photos filed to the patient record

    Clinical photos taken on a practice phone are shared to one intake, matched to the patient from the day's diary by time and chair, confirmed by the nurse, and filed as attachments through the Cliniko API or to a per-patient folder your system imports.

    Where a person decides: Unmatched photos wait in a queue for the nurse to assign.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Notes audited for missing fields

    Signed notes, from the Dentally API or a notes export, are read each night by a model on your own GPU against your list of required items, such as consent, medical history update and treatment code, and the gaps are listed per clinician.

    Where a person decides: The clinical lead sets the list and reviews the gaps.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Healthcare clinics · Insurance claims for US practices

Still keying claims in by hand and finding the rejections a month later?

  1. Claims drafted from the completed treatment

    For US practices. Completed treatments from the Open Dental API or the Dentrix or Eaglesoft export become claim forms with CDT codes, dates and fees filled in and checked against the payer rules you give me, before anyone submits.

    Where a person decides: The claims administrator reviews and submits. Nothing is submitted by the system.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Remittances and EOBs read and matched to claims

    Remittance advices and EOBs arriving as PDFs or forwarded email are read, matched to the claims they pay, and shortfalls, denials and partial payments are listed with the reason code and the next step.

    Where a person decides: The administrator decides what to resubmit, appeal or write off.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Eligibility checked through your clearinghouse before the visit

    US practices only. The day before each appointment, coverage and remaining benefits are checked through a clearinghouse with an eligibility API, such as DentalXChange or Availity, and gaps appear on the day sheet.

    Where a person decides: The desk calls the patient; the system only flags.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Claims pipeline: validated, submitted, aged by payer

    Claim drafting and remittance matching as one pipeline, plus what is new: every claim validated before it goes, submitted through the clearinghouse API, every payment reconciled to the fee, every denial queued, and an ageing report by payer. A month running in parallel first.

    Where a person decides: Your practice manager runs the first month in parallel and signs off before the old process is retired.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Claim forms and remittances are the document-intelligence shape: 412 records, 0 validation errors, on tournament data rather than claims.

Healthcare clinics · NHS contract for UK practices

Still finding out in March that the UDA target will not be hit?

  1. UDA delivery tracked weekly against the contract

    The UDA report export from SOE Exact or Dentally, or the Compass activity export, becomes one weekly table: delivered against expected to date, per performer, with the year-end projection at current pace and the shortfall it implies.

    Where a person decides: The practice owner reads it and decides on diary changes; the system changes nothing.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. FP17 claims checked before submission

    Unsubmitted courses of treatment in the practice system export are checked against your rules: band against treatment items, exemption evidence recorded, open courses past your cut-off, missing dates. A list per performer, before the claims leave.

    Where a person decides: The practice manager fixes and submits in the practice system; nothing is submitted by the system.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. BSA schedule reconciled to submitted claims

    The monthly pay schedule and activity statement from Compass, as PDF or CSV, are read and matched claim by claim to what was submitted. Rejections, reductions and late claims are listed with the reason, and the differences are totalled.

    Where a person decides: The practice manager decides what to resubmit; the bookkeeper posts the totals.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Patient charges reconciled to the schedule

    Patient charges collected per course of treatment, from the practice system export, against the patient charge revenue the schedule deducts. Shortfalls, exemptions claimed without evidence and charges taken at the wrong band are listed monthly.

    Where a person decides: The bookkeeper reviews the list; nothing is adjusted in the practice system.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: E-commerce Fulfillment ETL. 68,890 orders reconciled column by column for a retailer's finance team, synced nightly. Fulfilment data, not NHS claims.

Healthcare clinics · Lab work, stock and suppliers

Still tracking crowns and aligners on a whiteboard and checking lab invoices from memory?

  1. Lab cases tracked from send to fit

    Each lab ticket is logged when the impression or scan is sent, with the lab and the fit date from the diary. If the case is not back two days before the fit, the desk is warned and the lab is chased by email.

    Where a person decides: The nurse confirms arrival; the chase email uses a template you approve.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Lab invoices matched to patients and fees

    Lab invoices arriving as PDFs or forwarded email are read, each line matched to the patient and treatment in the practice system export through name and date noise, and the lab fee compared with what the patient was charged.

    Where a person decides: Unmatched lines and price differences wait for the practice manager.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Supplier invoices checked, then posted to the books

    Invoices from Henry Schein, Dental Directory or any supplier become line items checked for totals, VAT and duplicates before posting to Xero or QuickBooks. Unit prices are stored per item, so a price rise on a regular item is flagged the week it lands.

    Where a person decides: The bookkeeper clears the flagged queue; nothing unmatched is posted.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Weekly reorder list from reorder points

    You set a reorder point and pack size per regular item. The nurse enters a weekly count on a phone form; items under their point go on a reorder list with the supplier and the last price from the invoice history.

    Where a person decides: The nurse or manager places the order; the list is a suggestion.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. The 8-tier fuzzy resolver matched names through OCR noise: 412 records, 0 validation errors. Tournament data, not lab invoices.

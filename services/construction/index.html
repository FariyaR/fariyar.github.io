Still typing up quotes at night from WhatsApp photos and voice notes?

  1. Quote drafted from WhatsApp photos and voice notes

    Photos, voice notes and messages from WhatsApp or email go into one intake. The system pulls out scope, quantities and site details and drafts the quote against your price list, in your template.

    Where a person decides: You approve every quote before it is sent.

  2. Quote follow-ups that stop on reply

    A sent quote gets a scheduled follow-up sequence in templates you signed off. A customer reply stops it and lands in your inbox.

  3. Quote filed to the job in your CRM

    Where a person decides: Nothing is deleted or merged without your say.

Still matching supplier invoices to jobs at month end, from a pile of photos?

  1. Invoices and delivery notes read into job costs

    Scanned invoices, delivery notes and photographed receipts are read by a vision model, tied to the right supplier and job with fuzzy matching, and checked against quantities and totals before anything is written.

    Where a person decides: Documents that fail a check wait in a queue with the reason. You clear the queue.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Delivery notes checked against the order

    Each delivery note is compared with the purchase order line by line. Short deliveries and price differences are flagged the same day, not at invoice time.

    Where a person decides: You decide what to query with the supplier; the system only flags.

    AI systems2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. 412 records, 0 validation errors on mixed PDFs and scans, with a hard validation gate. Built for tournament data, not construction.

Construction and trades · Quotes and estimates

Still typing up quotes at night from WhatsApp photos and voice notes?

  1. Quote drafted from WhatsApp photos and voice notes

    Photos, voice notes and messages sent to a WhatsApp Business number, your inbox or an upload form on your site land in one intake. Voice notes are transcribed, scope and quantities pulled out, and the quote drafted against your price list in your template.

    Where a person decides: You approve every quote before it is sent.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Quote follow-ups that stop on reply

    A sent quote gets a scheduled follow-up sequence in templates you signed off. A customer reply stops it and lands in your inbox.

    Where a person decides: You choose the templates and the schedule; a reply always goes to you.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Quote filed to the job in your CRM

    Every quote, its photos and the thread are filed against the customer and the job in Tradify, ServiceM8, Jobber or whatever you run, so nothing lives only in a phone.

    Where a person decides: Nothing is deleted or merged without your say.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Price book refreshed from paid invoices

    Once invoice capture is in place, each price-book item is linked to the last price you paid, matched from the captured invoice lines through merchants' wording differences. When a merchant price moves, the quoting rate is flagged for update.

    Where a person decides: You decide whether a rate changes; the system only proposes.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Same mechanics: messy photos and scans in, validated records out. The client was a tournament data platform, not a contractor.

Construction and trades · Supplier invoices and deliveries

Still matching supplier invoices to jobs at month end, from a pile of photos?

  1. Invoices and delivery notes read into job costs

    Scanned invoices, delivery notes and photographed receipts are read by a vision model, matched to supplier and job through OCR noise, checked on quantities and totals, then posted to Xero, QuickBooks or Sage Accounting, or as an import file for Sage 50.

    Where a person decides: Documents that fail a check wait in a queue with the reason. You clear it, or a site manager does, within limits you set.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Delivery notes checked against the order

    Where a purchase order was raised, each delivery note is compared with it line by line. Short deliveries and price differences are flagged the same day, not at invoice time.

    Where a person decides: You decide what to query with the supplier; the system only flags.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Purchase orders drafted from the quote

    Material lines of a won quote are grouped by the usual supplier in your price list and drafted as purchase orders in Xero or QuickBooks, or as a numbered PDF for suppliers you phone. Delivery notes and invoices then have an order to match.

    Where a person decides: You pick the supplier and send each order. Nothing is ordered by the system.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Merchant statements reconciled to posted bills

    Monthly statements from builders' merchants and hire companies are matched line by line to the bills already posted. Missing invoices, duplicates and credits not received are listed with the job they belong to.

    Where a person decides: Your bookkeeper works the list; nothing is posted or written off by the system.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Plant hire tracked from hire and off-hire notes

    On-hire and off-hire notes from email, PDFs and a dedicated WhatsApp number are read into a live hire register per job, so equipment still on hire after the job closes is flagged before the invoice arrives.

    Where a person decides: The site manager confirms off-hire; the register only flags.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. 412 records, 0 validation errors on mixed PDFs and scans, with a hard validation gate. Built for tournament data, not construction.

Construction and trades · Job costing and reporting

Still finding out which jobs made money from the accountant, months later?

  1. Margin per job, refreshed every night

    Quotes, supplier costs and timesheets from Xero or QuickBooks, your job tool and your timesheet app, pulled nightly into SQL Server with margin per job. Reconciled against your accounting totals before anyone relies on it.

    Where a person decides: You and your bookkeeper sign off the reconciliation before cutover. The report is read-only.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Monday numbers in your inbox

    A short scheduled report: jobs quoted, won, in progress, invoiced and overdue, with the numbers linked to their source. No dashboard to log into.

    Where a person decides: You read it. The system changes nothing in your books.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Cost to complete per live job

    Committed orders, costs to date and the remaining budget per job, by cost code, refreshed from the same tables, so an overrun shows while the job is open. Needs cost codes and purchase orders in your systems; without them, start with margin per job.

    Where a person decides: The contracts manager decides what to do; the view only shows.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Power BI across jobs, sites and trades

    Dashboards on the fact tables: margin by job type, labour against estimate, supplier spend by trade and retention outstanding, with scheduled refresh so the number on screen is the number in the database.

    Where a person decides: You decide the metrics; definitions are written down once.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Estimate against actual, by trade and cost code

    When a job closes, the estimate is compared with what the job cost in labour and materials, by trade and cost code, since that is how costs are booked. Codes that are consistently under-priced are listed by job type.

    Where a person decides: You decide whether to change the rates; the comparison only informs.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: E-commerce Fulfillment ETL. 68,890 orders reconciled across 25 audited columns for a retailer's finance team. Same reconciliation work, different industry.

Construction and trades · Tenders and take-offs

Still reading tender packs page by page and counting quantities off drawings by hand?

  1. Tender pack summarised to scope, dates and risks

    The ITT, specification, preliminaries and contract terms are read into a fixed summary: scope by trade, submission deadline, site constraints, payment terms, liquidated damages and anything unusual, each item linked to its page.

    Where a person decides: The estimator reads the summary and decides whether to bid. The pack is never skipped.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Schedules and quantity tables read off tender drawings

    Door, window, finishes and sanitaryware schedules, and quantity tables printed on the drawings, are read by a vision model into a quantities list per element, each tied to its sheet. Nothing is counted or measured off the plan itself; that stays in your take-off tool.

    Where a person decides: The estimator checks every count against the drawing before it is priced.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Bill of quantities priced from your rate library

    A BoQ in Excel or CITE format is matched item by item to your rate library and the last three jobs that used it, with unmatched items listed for the estimator.

    Where a person decides: The estimator prices the unmatched items and signs off the bill. No rate is applied without a match.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Tender questions and addenda tracked

    Clarifications sent, answers received and addenda issued are logged against the tender from your tender inbox and the portal's notification emails, with the deadline moved when an addendum moves it.

    Where a person decides: The bid lead decides what to ask; the log only records.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Subcontractor quotes compared on one sheet

    Quotes from subcontractors arrive as PDFs in different layouts. Each is read into the same line structure, exclusions and provisional sums are called out, and a comparison sheet is built per package.

    Where a person decides: You choose the subcontractor; the sheet shows what each quote leaves out.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Vision extraction from mixed PDFs and scans with a hard validation gate: 412 records, 0 errors. Tournament data, not drawings or tender packs.

Construction and trades · Site diaries and progress

Still writing up the site diary on Friday from a week of WhatsApp photos?

  1. Daily site diary drafted from a site number

    The site sends photos, voice notes and messages to a dedicated WhatsApp Business number (group chats cannot be read). Each evening they become a dated diary entry per job: who was on site, work done, deliveries, weather, delays, filed to Procore, Fieldwire or your drive.

    Where a person decides: The site manager reads and signs the entry each day. Nothing is filed unsigned.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Progress photos sorted by job, area and date

    Each photo is filed to job, area and element from its caption, who sent it and what a vision model sees, then stored in folders you can search. Caption leads; vision alone does not file.

    Where a person decides: A person confirms low-confidence matches once; the rule is then remembered.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Snagging list built from photos and voice notes

    Defects captured on the walk-round as photos and voice notes, sent to the site number or logged in Fieldwire, become a numbered snag list with location, trade and description, assigned to the subcontractor and closed with a photo.

    Where a person decides: The site manager assigns and closes each snag. The list never closes one itself.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Variations caught before they are forgotten

    Messages to the site number and emails that read like a change to scope are flagged, a variation record is drafted with the instruction, the date and the photos, and it waits to be priced.

    Where a person decides: The contracts manager prices it and decides whether to claim it. Nothing is sent to the client.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Hours on site read into timesheets

    Hours sent as a WhatsApp message, a photo of the sheet or a clock-in app export are read per person per job per day, checked against site-access logs where you have them, and passed to payroll.

    Where a person decides: Mismatches wait for the site manager. Payroll never receives an unchecked hour.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Progress against programme, from diary and photos

    Diary entries and photos are tied to activities in your programme, read from an Asta Powerproject, MS Project (MPP) or Primavera P6 (XER) export, not a live link. Each activity shows last evidence of work and days of drift; a weekly client report is drafted.

    Where a person decides: The project manager updates the programme and sends the report. The system only proposes status.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. Scheduled narrative from several live sources, every line linked to its origin: the analytics agent, built for a marketing team, not a site.

Still finding out the site built off a superseded drawing after the fact?

  1. Drawing register kept current from issued PDFs

    Drawings arrive by issue email, as a download from Procore or Autodesk Construction Cloud, or in a shared folder. Number, title, revision and date are read from each title block, the register updated, superseded sheets flagged and the site told which drawings changed.

    Where a person decides: The document controller confirms each new revision before the site is told.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Superseded drawings on site caught from a photo

    A phone photo of any sheet in the site cabin is read for its number and revision and checked against the register. Out-of-date sheets are flagged back by message, with the current revision named. Needs the register above.

    Where a person decides: The site manager decides what to do with the sheet; the reply only flags.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. RFIs and submittals logged with an owner

    RFIs and technical submittals raised or answered by email, or through Procore or Aconex notification emails, are logged against the job with date raised, who owes the answer, days open and the drawings affected. A weekly open list goes to the project manager.

    Where a person decides: The project manager chases; the log only records and reminds.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Handover pack assembled from filed documents

    Test certificates, commissioning sheets, warranties, product data and as-built drawings already filed per job are read, matched to the handover index you use, and assembled into the O&M pack, with missing items listed per subcontractor so they can be chased before practical completion.

    Where a person decides: The project manager checks the pack and issues it. Nothing is sent to the client unreviewed.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Title blocks and certificates are the same job as the tournament scans: vision extraction, resolver, validation gate. Built for tournament data, not construction.

  3. Method statements reviewed against your checklist

Still building the monthly payment application from spreadsheets and forgetting the retentions?

  1. Monthly application assembled from the job record

    Work done to date, agreed variations and materials on site are pulled into your application template, with the previous certified amount carried forward. It draws on the site diary, the variation register and the cost tables, so those are built or exported first.

    Where a person decides: The quantity surveyor edits the amounts and submits. Nothing goes to the client unreviewed.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Payment certificates and notices read and compared

    Payment certificates and pay-less notices arrive as PDFs. Each is read, the certified amount compared line by line with what was applied for, and the differences listed with the reason given.

    Where a person decides: You decide what to dispute and by when. The deadline is shown, never acted on.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Retention ledger, held and owed, with release dates

    Retention held by clients on your applications and retention you hold on subcontractors, per job and party, in one ledger from Xero or QuickBooks, or a Sage export, plus the certificates and applications already read, with release dates and a reminder drafted when one passes.

    Where a person decides: You send the reminder and release the money. The ledger only shows and drafts.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Certified amounts chased past the final payment date

    When a certified amount is not paid by the final date for payment on the certificate, a chase is drafted in your words with the certificate reference and amount, and any notice deadline shown. The sequence stops when the payment lands in your bank feed.

    Where a person decides: A person approves every chase. Replies land with you, not the system.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Cash flow by job, twelve weeks out

    Applications due, certified amounts, supplier bills due and payroll, by week and by job, from the same reconciled tables, so the gap shows before the bank does.

    Where a person decides: You decide what to delay or chase. The forecast only shows.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Construction and trades · Enquiries and site visits

Still losing jobs because the enquiry came in while you were on site?

  1. Enquiries qualified and answered from one queue

    Web form (with photo upload), WhatsApp Business number, missed-call voicemail and email enquiries land in one queue. Each gets a reply drafted from your FAQ with the questions you always ask, and a lead in Jobber, ServiceM8 or your CRM.

    Where a person decides: You approve every first reply. Anything that reads like a complaint goes to you untouched.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Site visits booked from your calendar

    Once a lead is qualified, visit slots are offered from your calendar, the booking is confirmed by WhatsApp or email, and a reminder goes the day before.

    Where a person decides: You set the hours and the travel buffer; you can move any visit.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Missed calls answered by text, logged as leads

    A missed call to your business number triggers a text in your words asking what the job is and offering a callback time, with the number logged as a lead. Needs a VoIP or Twilio number, not a personal mobile.

    Where a person decides: You make the call back. The text only holds the lead.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Service reminders for annual work

    For businesses running on spreadsheets or plain accounting software rather than ServiceM8 or Tradify: customers whose boiler service, gutter clean or safety check falls due are messaged on schedule from your job history, with a booking link, and the chase stops on reply.

    Where a person decides: You pick which job types qualify and the wording.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Qualifying against written criteria and drafting replies behind a QA gate is the LeadForge pattern, built for B2B outreach, not a trades inbox.

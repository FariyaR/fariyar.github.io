Manufacturing and wholesale · Order intake

Still retyping purchase orders from PDF into the ERP, line by line?

  1. Purchase orders read from PDF and email

    Emailed PDFs and scans in, validated order lines out. Customer part numbers are matched to your catalogue; SKUs, quantities and prices are checked before posting to SAP Business One, Business Central, NetSuite or Odoo. An order-desk queue screen is an option.

    Where a person decides: Orders that pass every check post automatically. Anything that fails waits for your order desk with the reason.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Order confirmations sent from the ERP

    Once an order posts, the confirmation with lead time and delivery date goes back to the customer from a template, and the thread is filed.

    Where a person decides: A person approves confirmations for new customers and anything over a limit you set.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. EDI exceptions cleared, not retyped

    Retailer orders that fail in your EDI provider's exception queue (SPS Commerce, TrueCommerce) on an unknown part, wrong price or missing ship-to are matched against your catalogue and customer master, and the corrected order is re-released. Generating EDI yourself is a separate project.

    Where a person decides: Price and ship-to corrections are confirmed by the order desk before the order is re-released.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. B2B web orders mapped to ERP customers

    Where the standard Shopify or Amazon connector to Business Central or SAP B1 stops: orders matched to the right ERP customer and contract price list, and every order the connector rejects worked from the failed-sync log with the reason.

    Where a person decides: New customers and orders over a value you set wait for a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Customer forecasts and call-offs loaded as demand

    Weekly forecast spreadsheets and call-off schedules from key customers, each in its own layout, mapped once to your part numbers and loaded as demand into Business Central, Odoo or your planning sheet on every arrival.

    Where a person decides: A changed layout is confirmed by a planner before the new mapping is saved.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. Messy PDFs and scans into validated records: 412 records, 0 validation errors. Tournament data, not purchase orders; same shape, different industry.

Manufacturing and wholesale · RFQs and quoting

Still leaving RFQs in the inbox until someone has time to check stock and prices?

  1. Quote drafted from the RFQ

    Stock items only. An RFQ arrives by email or PDF; the quote is drafted from the customer's contract price, tier breaks, MOQs, stock and lead times in your ERP, in your template, lines under your floor margin flagged, and filed in HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive.

    Where a person decides: A person approves every quote before it is sent and decides any line below the floor.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Quote follow-ups and win/loss filed

    Open quotes get a follow-up sequence that stops on reply. Outcomes are written back to the CRM so the pipeline is real, not remembered.

    Where a person decides: You set the cadence; replies land with a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Drawings and specs read into a quote pack

    Customer drawings and specifications are read for title-block data, BOM tables, materials, tolerances and notes into a fixed spec sheet, and the closest past jobs are pulled from your ERP with their actual cost. The estimator builds the routing from there.

    Where a person decides: No operation is derived from geometry. The estimator sets every operation and the margin.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Quote turnaround and win rate reported

    Quotes sent, hours to first response, won and lost by rep, customer and product family, from the CRM and ERP, in a weekly report with every number linked to its source. Quick only if the CRM already records outcomes.

    Where a person decides: The sales manager reads it. The report changes nothing in the CRM.

    Data systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Portal RFQ notifications logged to deadline

    RFQ notification emails from Ariba, Coupa, Jaggaer and customer portals are read into one log with the deadline, the buyer and any attached documents, then assigned. Nothing is scraped from the portal; documents not in the email are downloaded by a person.

    Where a person decides: You decide which to bid on; the log only records.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. Drafting behind a QA gate with capped retries is the LeadForge pattern, built for outreach rather than quotes.

Manufacturing and wholesale · Warehouse to finance

Still explaining why the warehouse, the ERP and finance show three different totals?

  1. Warehouse and order data into SQL Server, nightly

    Ingestion from your WMS, ERP and order channels (ShipHero, Business Central, SAP B1, Unleashed, Cin7) into staging tables, then stored procedures building silver and fact tables, so every transformation is inspectable in SQL.

    Where a person decides: Your finance or QC team signs off a column-by-column parity audit before the new numbers replace the old ones.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Power BI datasets that are not a spreadsheet

    Shipments, stock movements and margins served as datasets Power BI reads directly, refreshed on schedule, with failures logged rather than hidden.

    Where a person decides: Finance reviews differences weekly; nothing is averaged away.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Month-end pack built on schedule

    The exports and pivot tables rebuilt by hand every month become a scheduled job that lands in the inbox with the numbers linked to their source.

    Where a person decides: The controller reads and signs off; the job never posts an adjustment.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Stock valuation that agrees with the ledger

    Built on the nightly tables above. Quantity on hand by location and lot from the WMS, costed at the ERP's method, compared with the inventory account each night. Differences listed by SKU with the movement that caused them.

    Where a person decides: Finance posts any adjustment by hand; the job never does.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Shipped, invoiced and paid matched per order

    Every sales order followed from despatch note to invoice to payment across the WMS, ERP and bank feed, so shipped-not-invoiced and invoiced-not-shipped are listed daily instead of discovered at audit.

    Where a person decides: The controller works the list; nothing is invoiced or written off automatically.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Lot traceability answered from the same tables

    Lot and batch numbers carried through receipt, work order, stock movement and shipment in the fact tables, with the ERP and WMS as sources, so a recall question (which customers got lot X, which lots went into order Y) answers in minutes.

    Where a person decides: Quality runs the query and decides the recall scope. Gaps in lot capture are listed, not guessed.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week

Closest system I’ve shipped: E-commerce Fulfillment ETL. Warehouse platform, legacy system, finance team, parity audit: 68,890 orders reconciled across 25 audited columns. A US retailer, not a manufacturer.

Manufacturing and wholesale · Purchasing and supplier documents

Still three-way matching supplier invoices by hand from PDFs in the inbox?

  1. Order confirmations checked against the PO

    Supplier order confirmations arrive as PDFs and emails. Each is read and compared with the purchase order on quantity, price and promised date, and the ERP's expected receipt date is updated when it moves.

    Where a person decides: Price and quantity differences wait for the buyer. Dates update automatically unless you say otherwise.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Shipping notices and packing lists into goods receipt

    ASNs, packing lists and container manifests are read into expected receipts per PO line, with lot numbers and quantities, so the warehouse books in against a list instead of a pallet. Built for container imports.

    Where a person decides: Goods receipt is confirmed by the warehouse; the list only prepares it.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Supplier invoices three-way matched before posting

    Invoice PDFs matched to the PO and the goods receipt, line by line, with price, quantity, currency and tax checked. Clean matches post to the ERP; the rest queue with the reason.

    Where a person decides: Accounts payable clears the queue. Nothing unmatched is posted.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Supplier price lists mapped and loaded

    New price lists as Excel or PDF, each in the supplier's layout and item codes, are mapped to your supplier item codes, compared with current ERP prices and loaded with the effective date. Increases above a threshold are listed. Quick only for coded Excel lists.

    Where a person decides: The buyer approves the load. Sell prices are never changed by the system.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Landed cost built from freight and duty invoices

    Freight, duty and brokerage invoices are read and allocated across the receipts they cover by weight, value or volume, so the ERP's landed cost per item is based on real invoices, not a flat percentage.

    Where a person decides: Finance approves the allocation rule and each month's postings.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Reorder proposals that respect MOQ and container fill

    Where Business Central's planning worksheet stops: a Monday reorder list per SKU from sales velocity, open orders, stock on hand and supplier lead time in the fact tables, rounded to each supplier's MOQ and filled to the container or pallet.

    Where a person decides: The buyer places the orders. The list is a proposal.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. PDFs and scans resolved through OCR noise with an 8-tier matcher and a hard gate: 412 records, 0 errors. Tournament data, not supplier paperwork.

Manufacturing and wholesale · Quality and inspection

Still filing certificates of analysis unread and writing up NCRs after the shift?

  1. Certificates of analysis checked against the spec

    For food, chemicals and pharma. Supplier CoAs and test reports arriving as PDFs are read per lot, each value compared with your specification limits, and the lot released or held in Business Central, SAP B1 or your QMS with the result attached.

    Where a person decides: Out-of-spec lots are held for the quality manager. Releasing a held lot is a person's decision.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Non-conformance reports drafted from photos and notes

    A photo and a voice note sent from the line become a drafted NCR with part, lot, defect type, quantity and where it was found, filed in your QMS (Qualio, ETQ or a SharePoint list) and linked to the supplier or work order.

    Where a person decides: The quality engineer edits and signs the NCR. Disposition is theirs.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Labels and packaging photos checked in batch

    Photos of labels, packaging and shipping marks taken at the end of the line are checked in batch against written rules: right label on the right lot, legible barcode, correct language. Failures are parked for review. Not inline inspection; no camera, lighting or PLC work.

    Where a person decides: An operator decides on every parked photo. Rules are yours and can be changed any time.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Supplier quality scorecard from receipts and NCRs

    On-time delivery, rejected lots and NCRs per supplier from the ERP and QMS, in a monthly scorecard, every number linked to the receipt or report behind it.

    Where a person decides: Purchasing decides what to do with a supplier; the scorecard only shows.

    Data systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Audit evidence assembled for ISO 9001

    Calibration records, training records, NCR closures and management review minutes are collected from where they live into the folder structure your auditor expects, with gaps listed before the audit, not during it.

    Where a person decides: The quality manager fills the gaps and signs off the pack.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  6. Root causes grouped across NCRs and complaints

    NCR and complaint text is clustered by defect, part family and supplier each month, so the same failure reported ten different ways shows as one line with the records behind it.

    Where a person decides: The quality team decides which to open a CAPA on.

    AI systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Creative Generation Pipeline. A vision model checking every item against written rules, verdict and rule recorded, 100+ a day. Built for ad creatives, not factory labels.

Still retyping the shift report and guessing OEE from a paper downtime log?

  1. Shift reports read from paper and WhatsApp

    Shift-sheet photos, voice notes and messages from supervisors to a WhatsApp Business number or Teams are read into output, downtime, scrap and reasons per line and shift, checked against counter totals from your MES or the machine-data tables below, and written to a shared table.

    Where a person decides: A mismatch with the counters waits for the supervisor.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Machine data into SQL Server for OEE

    Counts, states and alarms from CSV drops or your MES API first; OPC UA through a Kepware or Ignition gateway, never direct to PLCs. Staging, stored procedures building run, stop and scrap tables, and Power BI for OEE by line and shift.

    Where a person decides: Production signs off the parity check against the manual log before the old sheet is retired.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week
  3. Morning production digest before the meeting

    Yesterday's output, downtime by reason, scrap and open work orders in one short message to Teams or Slack at a set time, every number linked to its source.

    Where a person decides: The production manager reads it and decides. The agent changes nothing.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Work instructions answered from your own documents

    Operators ask a question in Teams or on a tablet and get an answer drawn only from your work instructions, drawings and machine manuals, with the page it came from. A self-hosted model is an option where drawings must stay on site.

    Where a person decides: Answers cite the document. Anything about safety or a deviation is routed to a supervisor.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Run hours fed into the CMMS

    Run hours and cycle counts from the machine-data tables are written to the asset meters in MaintainX, Fiix or Limble through their API, so the meter-based work orders those tools already raise trigger on real hours, not on a calendar. Needs the machine-data service.

    Where a person decides: The maintenance planner schedules the stop. The work order is the CMMS's own proposal.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks

    Where a person decides: The planner moves the work. The view warns; it never reschedules an order.

    Data systemsComplex system · multi-week
  2. Engineering change impact list from the ECO

    An ECO as PDF, or an export from SolidWorks PDM or Autodesk Vault, is read for affected parts and revisions, and every BOM, routing, drawing and work instruction that references them is listed from the ERP API and your document store, plus drafted work-instruction edits.

    Where a person decides: The engineer releases every BOM and routing change. The list and the drafts never write to the ERP.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Standard times checked against actual cycle times

    Standard times in the ERP routing compared with actual run and setup times from the machine-data tables, per part and work centre, so the standards behind costing and lead-time promises are listed where wrong, with the runs behind each line. Needs the machine-data service.

    Where a person decides: The estimator and the controller read it. Rates and margins change only when they say so.

    Data systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks

Still answering “where is my order?” by opening the ERP and the carrier site?

    Status emails and WhatsApp Business messages are matched to the order, the ERP and the carrier API (UPS, DHL, FedEx) are checked, and a reply is drafted with the despatch date, tracking link and any backorder lines.

    Where a person decides: A person approves each reply until you choose to let routine ones go out alone.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Late orders flagged before the customer asks

    Each morning, orders past their promised date or short on stock are listed by customer, and a notice in your wording is drafted with the new date from the ERP.

    Where a person decides: Sales decides which customers to tell and when. Nothing is sent without approval.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Returns and warranty claims logged with photos

    RMA requests arriving by email, web form or WhatsApp Business are matched to the order and lot, photos are checked against your return rules, and an RMA is raised in the ERP with a drafted acknowledgement.

  4. Carrier claims filed from PODs and photos

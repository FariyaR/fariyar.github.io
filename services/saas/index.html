Still shipping an AI feature that works in the demo and times out on the first real customer?

  1. Queued jobs and polling instead of held requests

    A job API and a worker as separate services: jobs are queued and polled, not held open until they time out, and the expensive part scales on its own.

    Where a person decides: Every job is tracked from upload to result with status and timing, so your team can audit a run before it ships.

    AI products4–8 weeks
  2. Self-hosted models where data must stay

    Open-weight models on your own GPUs, EU-resident and hardened where residency matters, so the enterprise security review does not stall on a third-party API.

    Where a person decides: You sign off the architecture sketch before the build starts.

    AI products4–8 weeks
  3. Typed contracts on every boundary

    Model output validated into a fixed schema before the UI sees it, so a new edge case fails loudly in one place instead of shipping as a hotfix.

    Where a person decides: Your team owns the schema; changes are reviewed like any code.

    AI products4–8 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: DentaSmile AI. A live three-service SaaS with self-hosted GPU inference: ~110 s per job, queued and polled, and the app does not break.

SaaS and software companies · Agent prototype rescue

Still demoing an agent that loops on the third run?

  1. Prototype rebuilt with one typed shared state

    The prototype rebuilt as a graph with one typed shared state instead of message passing, validated outputs at every agent boundary, a QA agent, and retries with correction capped at two attempts.

    Where a person decides: Outputs that fail QA twice are parked for a person with the reason. Nothing loops or fails silently.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Provider-agnostic model layer

    Models swappable per agent without touching orchestration, with tests over the graph and streaming so reviewers see progress.

    Where a person decides: Your team chooses models per step and can change them any time.

    AI systems2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: LeadForge AI. 6 agents, one shared state, retries visible in the UI; a failing prototype was rebuilt the same way for a US client.

Still returning free text from uploads and fixing the UI with hotfixes?

  1. Extraction into your own schema, validated after the model

    PDFs, scans and messy uploads extracted into your schema with deterministic validation after the model: the model proposes, rules decide. Built into your existing app.

    Where a person decides: Records that fail the gate are queued for your team or your users to resolve.

    AI systems2–6 weeks
  2. Entity resolution instead of one threshold

    An escalating matcher built for OCR noise, so names and references resolve reliably inside your product.

    Where a person decides: Confidence thresholds are set with you and visible to users where it matters.

    AI systems2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Tournament Document Intelligence. 412 records, 0 validation errors; the Upwork review on the same work notes the solution was implemented inside the client’s own app.

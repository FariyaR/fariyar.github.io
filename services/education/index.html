Still building every enrolment ad by hand, capped by one designer’s calendar?

  1. Creative generated and checked against your brand rules

    Brief, cached prompt, image generation, then a vision model checks each image against written brand rules and regenerates on failure, capped. Runs in n8n on a schedule.

    Where a person decides: A creative that fails twice is parked. Your team approves the final set before it goes to Meta Ads.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Testimonials and course visuals at volume

    Quote cards, module graphics and launch assets produced from the same rules and templates, so a launch does not wait on design.

    Where a person decides: You approve each launch set.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Ad copy variants drafted from the offer, QA-gated

    Headlines and primary text for Meta and Google Ads drafted from the course page, the offer and your voice guide, checked by a QA step against claims you have not allowed, retries capped.

    Where a person decides: The marketer picks the variants and loads them. Claims about outcomes or income never publish without a person.

    AI systemsQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Approved creatives pushed into Meta Ads

    Approved batches uploaded into Meta Ads through the Marketing API with names, ad set and tracking set from one naming rule, and the Airtable or sheet record updated with the ad ID.

    Where a person decides: A person approves the batch and sets the budget. The pipeline never turns a campaign on.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  5. Creative results written back to each image

    Spend, clicks and enrolments per creative from Meta Ads and GA4 joined to the generation record under a UTM naming rule agreed first, so you can see which brief, prompt and rule produced the ads that enrolled.

    Where a person decides: The team decides what to brief next; the table only shows.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: Creative Generation Pipeline. Built for an EU education brand and still running: 100+ QA-checked creatives a day at 78% lower inference cost.

Education, coaching and course businesses · Daily performance

Still logging into Kajabi, Meta and GA4 each morning to find out what sold?

  1. Morning performance digest

    An agent with tools over Kajabi, Meta, Google Ads and GA4 pulls the last 24 hours, reasons over them and posts one narrative message to Slack at a set time, every number linked to its source.

    Where a person decides: The agent writes the digest and changes nothing. The team decides from the message.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Launch tracker in one place

    Enrolments, ad spend, webinar registrations and attendance from Zoom or WebinarJam, checkout views and drop-off from Kajabi or ThriveCart, Meta and GA4 pulled into one dataset and reported daily until the cart closes, with the step losing most people named.

    Where a person decides: You decide what to change; the tracker only shows.

    Data systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  3. Anomaly alerts on spend and conversion

    Scheduled checks on Meta, Google Ads and GA4: cost per enrolment out of range, a campaign spending with no checkouts, a pixel that stopped firing. One alert to Slack with the number and its source.

    Where a person decides: A person pauses or changes anything. The checker never touches budgets.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  4. Ask-the-numbers agent in Slack

    A question typed in Slack is answered by an agent with read-only tools over Kajabi, Stripe, Meta, Google Ads and GA4, every figure linked and every tool call logged. Runs on the same hardened EU-resident server as the digest if you need residency.

    Where a person decides: Read-only tools. The agent cannot change a campaign, a price or a page.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks

Closest system I’ve shipped: OpenClaw Analytics Agent. OpenClaw’s four live integrations are Kajabi, GA4, Meta and Google Ads: one source-linked digest to Slack at 07:00, 24/7, on a hardened EU server.

Still answering “is this course right for me?” by hand, hours after it was asked?

  1. DMs and emails answered from your own FAQ

    Instagram DMs through ManyChat, WhatsApp and email enquiries qualified against your course criteria, replies drafted from your FAQ and pricing behind a QA step, follow-ups until the person answers. Leads land scored in HubSpot, ActiveCampaign or Kit.

    Where a person decides: Every reply is approved by a person. Questions the FAQ does not cover are handed to you with the draft.

    AutomationStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Abandoned checkouts and onboarding on schedule

    Kajabi, Teachable or ThriveCart webhooks drive the steps the platform’s own emails do not cover: WhatsApp checkout nudges, HubSpot or ActiveCampaign stages, cohort reminders and onboarding across tools, instead of a sheet someone remembers.

    Where a person decides: You own the sequences; replies reach a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Applications read and scored against written criteria

    Cohort or mastermind applications from Typeform or your form, with uploads, extracted into a fixed schema and scored against criteria written down once, with the evidence for each score shown beside it.

    Where a person decides: A person accepts or declines. The score is advice shown next to the application, not instead of it.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Discovery calls booked and briefed

    Qualified leads are offered Calendly or HubSpot slots and reminded, and the coach or closer gets a one-page brief before the call: who they are, what they asked, what they were sent.

    Where a person decides: The coach runs the call. Prices and discounts are never quoted by the system.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  5. Sales calls scored against your script

    Setter and closer calls from Zoom, Fathom or Fireflies transcripts scored against your written script, objections tagged, every score tied to a quoted line and checked by a QA agent, feedback drafted per rep and the deal note updated in HubSpot or Close.

    Where a person decides: The sales lead reads the feedback before a rep sees it. Nothing is sent to a rep by the system.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week

    Where a person decides: A moderator approves replies at first; you choose when routine answers may send alone.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  2. Progress nudges and check-ins on schedule

    Students who stall at a lesson or miss a live call get a message in your wording from Kajabi, Teachable or Thinkific and Zoom events. “Stopped logging in” comes from a scheduled pull of the platform’s progress report, not an event.

    Where a person decides: You own the wording and the rules; nudges stop on a reply, which goes to a person.

    AutomationQuick win · days to 2 weeks
  3. Stuck and disengaged students spotted early

    Posts and messages in Circle, Slack or Discord read daily through their APIs for stuck, frustrated or disengaged students, with a short list for the coach and the messages that triggered it. Skool is not covered: it has no API for posts.

    Where a person decides: The coach decides who to call. No student is messaged from this list by the system.

    AI systemsStandard build · 2–6 weeks
  4. Assignments graded against your rubric, feedback drafted

    Submissions from Google Drive, Typeform or forwarded email, PDFs, docs or scans, extracted and scored per rubric criterion with the evidence quoted, a QA agent rejecting any score without evidence, feedback drafted per student into a review screen. You enter grades in your platform.

    Where a person decides: The instructor approves or changes every grade and every line of feedback before a student sees it.

    AI systemsComplex system · multi-week
